A day at Cedrick's

Mornings are for coffee and calm water, afternoons for the reef, and evenings for dinner with your feet in the sand.

  • Sunrise and breakfastWatch the bay turn gold from the main beach. Breakfast is included with every room.
  • 8:00 AM and 1:00 PMIntro scuba dive with a certified instructor, or snorkel over the coral gardens.
  • MiddayIsland-hopping by boat: 3 stops, snorkel gear and a packed lunch, about 4 hours.
  • SunsetFour-course seafood dinner on the sand. Book one day ahead.
