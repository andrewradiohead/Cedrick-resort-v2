A family-run resort on a quiet cove

Cedrick's Resort opened in 2012 as six bamboo-and-nipa cottages built by the Cedrick family on land they had fished from for three generations. Today it has 24 rooms, an open-air restaurant and a small dive shop, and it is still managed by the family.

We sit within reach of the marine reserves and white-sand beaches that make Puerto Galera a UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve. Guests choose us for calm water, honest prices and staff who know the coast by name.

Mission

Give every guest a restful, safe and memorable coastal stay while protecting the reef that sustains our community.

Vision

The most trusted family resort in Oriental Mindoro, known for sincere hospitality and responsible tourism.

Location. Sabang–Balatero coastal road, Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro.

Target market. Couples, families, small barkadas, and certified or beginner divers from Metro Manila, Batangas and abroad.

What sets us apart. Sea-view rooms at mid-range prices, in-house dive shop, local-staffed island tours and a refill-only plastic policy.

Tourism value. We source seafood and produce from nearby fisherfolk and farms, and about 90% of our team lives within 5 km of the resort.

What we stand for

Responsible tourism

Refill stations instead of single-use bottles, reef-safe habits and clear rules on coral protection.

Local first

Seafood and produce come from nearby fisherfolk and farms. About 90% of the team lives within 5 km.

Safety always

Life vests on every boat trip, certified dive instructors and honest weather calls.

Family hospitality

Staff who know the coast by name and treat every guest like a visiting relative.

Fair prices

Mid-range rates with breakfast included and no hidden charges.

Community

Tours are led by local boatmen and guides, so tourism income stays in the community.

Beneath the surface

Our small dive shop runs intro dives and snorkel tours over the reefs and marine reserves that surround Puerto Galera.

  • Certified instructors and well-kept gear
  • Small groups so every guest gets attention
  • Pool briefing before your first dive
  • No diving within 18 hours of flying
