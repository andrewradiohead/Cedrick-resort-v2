Travel guide

Everything worth knowing before you pack.

Best time to visit

November to May is dry with calm seas. June to October is wetter and rougher; boat tours may be cancelled for safety.

What to bring

  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Swimwear, light cotton clothes, a light jacket
  • Refillable bottle, cash for local shops

Estimated budget

About ₱3,500 to ₱6,000 per person per day, including room share, meals and one activity.

Safety

  • Swim only in marked areas
  • Wear life vests on all boat trips
  • Do not dive within 18 hours of flying

Local customs and etiquette

Greet staff and villagers with a smile and "po" or "opo". Ask before photographing people. Cover up when leaving the beach area.

Environment

Do not touch or stand on coral. Take trash back to shore. We use refill stations, not single-use bottles.

Accessibility

Garden Cottages and the restaurant have step-free paths. Tell us your needs and we will prepare a ground-floor room.

Emergency information

Resort front desk: +63 917 555 0142 (24 hours). Emergency hotline: 911. Nearest health center: Puerto Galera Municipal Health Office.

Dress code

Beachwear is fine on the sand. Smart casual is appreciated at dinner. Shirts are required in the restaurant.

Safety references from local government

Check these official sources before and during your trip. Numbers and advisories can change, so confirm them first.

Oriental Mindoro PDRRMO

Provincial disaster risk reduction office. Operations and Warning Division: +63 948 146 0382 or +63 920 951 3690. Research, Planning, Administration and Training: +63 916 220 1847.

National emergency

Dial 911 for police, fire and medical emergencies anywhere in the Philippines.

Port and sea travel

The PDRRMO posts port status updates, such as Calapan Port passenger conditions. Check the province's situation updates and the Philippine Coast Guard before any boat trip.

Weather and sea conditions

Check PAGASA forecasts and weather advisories before island-hopping or diving. Follow local boatmen and resort staff if a trip is cancelled.

Water quality and local advisories

Follow advisories from the Puerto Galera local government, provincial government and health authorities on swimming areas and tourism operations.

Tourism information

The Department of Tourism publishes travel advisories and tourism guidance for the MIMAROPA region.

  • References: Provincial Government of Oriental Mindoro (ormindoro.gov.ph), PDRRMO situation updates
  • PAGASA weather · Philippine Coast Guard · Department of Tourism

Be a responsible traveler

  • Do not touch, stand on or collect coral and shells.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen and carry a refillable bottle.
  • Take your trash back to shore.
  • Ask before photographing people and respect local customs.
  • Follow your guide and boatman, especially on rough days.
