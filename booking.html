Good to know before you book

Deposit

A 30% deposit holds your dates.

Cancellation

Weather

Check-in and out

Promo

Reply time

What to include in your inquiry

  • Your preferred dates and number of guests
  • The room or package you like
  • Any special requests, such as a ground-floor room or a celebration dinner

Frequently asked questions

How do I confirm a booking?

Send the inquiry form. We reply within 24 hours with availability and payment instructions. A 30% deposit holds your dates.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Later cancellations forfeit the deposit.

Do you accept children and pets?

Children are welcome. Pets are not allowed inside rooms, but service animals are accepted.

What happens if the weather cancels my tour?

We reschedule at no cost or refund the activity fee.
