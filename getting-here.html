Getting to Cedrick's

We are about 130 km south of Manila by road and sea, roughly 4 to 5 hours door to door.

FromRouteTime
ManilaBus or van to Batangas Pier, then ferry to Balatero or Muelle Pier4 to 5 hours
Calapan CityVan or jeepney along the coastal road2.5 to 3 hours
Resort pickupAircon van or tricycle from the pier15 to 25 minutes

Nearby attractions

  • White Beach and Sabang for dining and nightlife
  • Tamaraw Falls and Ponderosa viewpoint
  • Mangyan cultural village visit
  • Coral gardens and marine reserve

Eating and staying nearby

Our restaurant serves breakfast to dinner. Local eateries and a minimart are within a 10-minute tricycle ride. Overflow rooms are available at partner inns during peak season.

Nearby attractions

White Beach and Sabang

Long sandy shore with dining, shops and nightlife a short ride away.

Tamaraw Falls

A cool waterfall pool, best visited in the morning and in the dry months.

Ponderosa viewpoint

Sweeping views over the bay and hills, lovely at sunrise or late afternoon.

Mangyan cultural village

Meet the community and learn about traditional crafts and way of life.

Tips for the journey

  • Leave Manila early so you catch a morning ferry from Batangas Pier.
  • Sea conditions can delay boats. Check port updates from the provincial government before you travel.
  • Bring some cash. ATMs in town can be offline.
  • Ask us to arrange an aircon van pickup (₱2,500 per van) from the pier.
